HOW TO READ THE RESULT

Sustainability evidence, with its limits

Material choices matter, but recycled or organic content alone doesn’t tell the whole story.

01 · MATERIALS

What the listing says

We identify the stated fibers and compare their likely tradeoffs using independent research. If the product page doesn’t disclose composition, the analysis should say so.

02 · CONFIDENCE

What evidence exists

The score reflects the completeness of supporting information, such as product-specific impact data, verifiable certifications, and repair details. It is not an environmental grade.

03 · ALTERNATIVES

What else to consider

When confidence is low, similar garments appear only if a product listing and separate supporting research back a named sustainability advantage.

!

Unknown does not mean unsustainable. Public product details may not disclose a garment’s full footprint or supply chain. Open the linked sources and treat alternatives as leads to compare, not guarantees of lower impact.
